# Tidiva · US App Store listing

**App name**  
Tidiva: Cleaning Planner

**Subtitle**  
Room-by-room chore schedules

**Promotional text**  
In the office Monday through Wednesday? Pick your rooms, set your available days, and give each cleaning task a place on your calendar.

**Description**

Open Tidiva to see what needs cleaning today and how long it will take. Choose a routine for a work-from-home break, an evening reset, or a visit from friends. You can also build one from scratch.

Plan room by room  
Walk through your home one floor at a time. Choose the rooms you want to tackle, pick suggested tasks, or add your own with an icon, a time estimate, and an optional note.

Fit chores around work  
Tell Tidiva which days you spend at the office and how much time you have for cleaning. Review your proposed schedule before saving it. If your plans change, adjust the days you want to keep free.

See what’s next  
Check off today’s tasks as you go or start a guided session. Browse another day in the calendar, add upcoming tasks to Apple Calendar, and remove those events from Tidiva whenever you like. Set two or three daily reminders at times that work for you.

Tidiva is free to use with ads. Premium includes ad-free sessions, 30 days of activity history, progress by room, and CSV export. Choose a monthly or annual subscription, or buy lifetime access with a single payment. Eligible subscribers can try Premium free for seven days. The price and billing period appear before purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically until canceled. Manage your plan from Settings → Subscription.
